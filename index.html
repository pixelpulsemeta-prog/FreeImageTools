Frequently Asked Questions

Are my images uploaded to any server?
No. All processing occurs 100% locally within your browser using client-side JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas APIs. Your photos never leave your device.
Is FreeImageTools completely free?
Yes, 100% free with unlimited usage. There are no subscriptions, hidden fees, paywalls, or daily quotas.
Do I need to install any software or extensions?
No installation is needed. All image compression, resizing, conversion, and cropping utilities work directly inside standard modern web browsers on Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS, and Android.
What image formats are supported?
FreeImageTools supports JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP files. You can convert seamlessly between formats, compress files to specific sizes, or resize to exact pixel specifications.
